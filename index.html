1. Information We Collect

1.1 Personal Information Stored on Our Servers (Google Firestore)

This is the only category of data that leaves your device permanently. It is used solely to create your account and generate your astrological profile:

  • Birth Details: Date, time, and place of birth (required for chart generation)
  • Account Information: Name, sign-in identifier (e.g., Google Sign-In)
  • Gender (if provided)
  • Engagement Data: Login streak count tied to your account

1.2 Automatically Collected Information

  • Device Information: Device model, OS version, unique device identifiers
  • Usage & Crash Data: Firebase Analytics and crash reporting, used only to fix bugs and improve the app
  • Approximate Location: Only if used for timezone/Panchang accuracy — not stored on our servers

1.3 Information Stored ONLY on Your Device (Never Sent to Us)

The following is generated and stored exclusively in local storage on your device and is never transmitted to or stored on our servers:

  • Daily and weekly horoscope content
  • Panchang (Vedic almanac) data
  • Birth charts (D1 Lagna/Rashi and D9 Navamsha), in North Indian, South Indian, and East Indian display styles
  • Match-making / compatibility check results and any partner details you enter
  • Your conversation history with the AI Astro chatbot
  • Your selected language preference (English, Hindi, or Gujarati)

Because this data never reaches our servers, it is permanently lost if you uninstall the App or clear its local storage — we cannot recover it.

1.4 AI Chatbot Processing

When you message AI Astro, your message text is sent in real time to our AI service provider solely to generate a reply, and is not retained by us afterward. The visible chat history is stored locally on your device as described in 1.3.
